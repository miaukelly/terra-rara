HOW TO PLAY

  • 7 geography prompts, easy to hard — countries, landmarks, rivers, islands, mountains and more. Give one answer each.
  • You get 25 seconds once the clock starts. No autocomplete — spelling is forgiving though.
  • Every correct answer drills, but the answers nobody else thinks of drill much deeper.
  • Rarity runs Tourist Trap → Beaten Path → Off the Map → Hidden Gem → Uncharted → Terra Incognita. The rarer the find, the harder the drill plunges.
  • Wrong guesses don't end the round — keep digging until the clock runs out.
  • A perfect game reaches the centre of the Earth: 6,371 km.
